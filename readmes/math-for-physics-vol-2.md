## Summary

*Mathematics for Physics, Vol. 2: Advanced Tools* is a structured textbook for readers who need a clear path through the subject rather than a loose collection of explanations. It begins with Logarithms, Exponentials, and Scales, moves through 11 core chapters, and ends with Calculus of Variations and the Lagrangian/Hamiltonian Formalism.

AI matters to *Mathematics for Physics, Vol. 2: Advanced Tools* because the modern textbook is no longer only a static container. It is also part of a learning system: searchable, remixable, explainable, and increasingly connected to tools such as Medhavy. For Humanitarians AI books, the relevant question is not whether AI can replace the learner or the teacher. It cannot. The useful question is what AI can make easier to inspect: definitions, worked examples, misconceptions, practice sequences, alternate explanations, and the structure of an argument. This book treats AI as infrastructure for open, public-interest learning infrastructure. The chapters should still stand on their own as readable prose, but they are also designed to be legible to an intelligent textbook system.

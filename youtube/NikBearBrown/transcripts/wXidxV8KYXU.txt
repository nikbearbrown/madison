In the wild forests of the southwandered a rockshasi, a demoness namedSharpanaka, sister to the demon kingRaana, born of a sage and a rockshasi.Widowed by her own brother's hand, sheroamed the wild woods of Dandaka. Evenher name was a warning, whispered infear across the southern forests. Thenshe saw the exiled prince Rama, andsmitten took on a beautiful form to winhim. He refused her, his brother refusedher. Enraged, she flew at Rama's wife,Ceda. Humiliated, she fled to Raana andinflamed him with tales of Ceda'sbeauty. And so began the great war ofLanka, a minor character, yet the sparkof the whole epic. And to later poets,no monster at all. Shanaka, the scorned,disfigured woman whose wound set godsand demons to war, remembered now asvillain, as victim, and as somethingachingly human.
